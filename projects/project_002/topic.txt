“Hey dads… or papas… may I have your attention for a minute?”
“Between the ages of 3 and 7, your child is trying to answer one big question…”\
‘Who am I?’

This is the age when children suddenly start saying:

“No.”
“I’ll do it myself.”
“I don’t need help.”

And many parents think the child has become difficult.

But actually…

they’re discovering their individuality.

This is where father energy becomes very important.

Father energy helps a child learn:

How to take space.
How to try things.
How to express themselves in the world.

⸻

But here is something very interesting.

Children don’t just learn this from what fathers say.

They learn it from how fathers live their life.

⸻

If a child sees a father who is genuinely engaged in his work…

Who enjoys what he does…

Who pursues things with passion instead of just surviving…

The child learns something very powerful without a single word.

⸻

They learn that adulthood is not just responsibility.

It’s also self-expression.

And when that father energy is present and authentic…

The child feels safer discovering their own identity.

Even the tantrums of this age pass with less struggle.

And if the father is not around…

This energy can still come from other masculine role models.

A grandfather.
A mentor.
A coach.
A teacher.

Because every child needs someone who reflects back their individuality.

Ending line

“Mother energy teaches the child the world is safe.”

“Father energy teaches the child…”
