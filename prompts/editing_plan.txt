## Rules
- Beats must cover the full script in time order. Each beat has start and end_seconds (cumulative timeline).
- Every beat (aroll or broll) must be aligned to the **exact lines being spoken** in that time range, not generic ideas from elsewhere in the script.
- type "aroll" = show the speaker (recorded video). Max 3-5 seconds before cutting to broll or a zoom cut. Follow the B-Roll Ratio and Editing Pacing rules from the Reference Material.

Every broll beat MUST have "suggestion" using this exact format: "[Subject performing an action]. Camera: [one clear camera move]. [Scene/lighting/environment detail]."
- B-roll suggestions must be **literal, concrete visuals** that clearly depict what the viewer just heard or is hearing: real-world parents, children, households, and astrology artifacts (charts, constellations, sky, symbols). - Filler words (um, uh) will be detected later and covered with a neutral B-roll clip + muted audio automatically.
